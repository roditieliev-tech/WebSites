Data Engineering

Custom Python ETL & Lakehouse Pipelines

Modern enterprises sit atop fragmented data silos spread across operational databases, SaaS platforms, and legacy flat files. Without a disciplined ingestion layer, analytics teams make decisions on stale or incomplete data, finance runs month-end close on manually reconciled spreadsheets, and engineering spends weekends debugging silent schema drift. Rixvol replaces these fragile scripts with production-grade, vectorized Python pipelines.

Our pipelines are built on Polars and PyArrow for SIMD-accelerated execution — achieving 15x to 30x throughput gains over legacy Pandas workflows. Combined with Change Data Capture replication, strict Pydantic schema contracts, and ACID-compliant warehouse commits, we deliver zero-silent-data-corruption guarantees from day one.

When You Need This
Legacy ETL is a ticking time bomb
Your Pandas/Airflow scripts take hours to run, silently drop malformed records, and no one dares refactor them because the original author left two years ago.
Multi-source data consolidation nightmares
Finance, ops, and product each have their own database with conflicting schemas. Month-end reconciliation is a manual, week-long ordeal that still produces errors.
Analytics team can't trust the warehouse
Dashboards show different numbers depending on when they were loaded. Schema drift breaks downstream models without warning, and nobody finds out until the board meeting.
Polars & PyArrow SIMD Core
Vectorized Rust-backed execution and zero-copy Arrow memory layouts for 15x–30x faster throughput than Pandas.
CDC & Incremental Replication
Low-impact Change Data Capture from PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MongoDB with watermarks for exact incremental syncs.
Schema Contracts & DLQ
Pydantic v2 and Pandera validation automatically quarantines malformed payloads without halting production runs.
Idempotent Lakehouse Commits
Deterministic hash deduplication and ACID transactions across Iceberg, Delta Lake, ClickHouse, and BigQuery.

Distributed Streaming

Real-Time Event Streaming Systems

Batch-processed data is yesterday's data. When your fraud detection sees transactions 15 minutes late, when your operations dashboard refreshes every hour, or when your users wait until tomorrow to see their latest activity — you're leaving money and trust on the table. Real-time is not a luxury; for fintech, logistics, and IoT, it's the minimum bar.

Rixvol architects fault-tolerant Apache Kafka and Redpanda event buses paired with stateful stream processors. We compute rolling metrics, detect anomalies, and push live telemetry to dashboards in single-digit milliseconds — with exactly-once delivery semantics and automatic backpressure protection.

When You Need This
Fraud is slipping through the cracks
Your risk engine sees transactions minutes after they happen. By the time a suspicious pattern is flagged, the damage is done and chargebacks are piling up.
Operations fly blind between ETL runs
Fleet positions, inventory levels, or server health are only updated hourly. Decisions are made on stale data, and incidents escalate before anyone notices.
Downstream systems cascade-fail under load
A traffic spike overwhelms your consumers, messages back up, and the entire pipeline grinds to a halt — taking your analytics and alerts with it.
Kafka & Redpanda Clusters

Applied AI & Cognitive Data Pipelines

LLMs are powerful — but in production, they're unpredictable. Without guardrails, your document parsing pipeline hallucinates invoice totals, your classification system invents categories, and your RAG chatbot confidently cites documents that don't exist. The gap between a ChatGPT demo and a production AI system is an engineering discipline, not a prompt.

Rixvol embeds LLMs and embedding models into production pipelines with deterministic schema guardrails, audit trails, and cost controls. We use constrained decoding, Pydantic verification loops, and mathematical cross-checks to eliminate hallucinations — while semantic caching and tiered model routing slash inference spend by up to 74%.

When You Need This
Document processing is drowning your team

Your LLM prototype can't go to production

Institutional knowledge is locked in documents
Your team wastes hours searching through thousands of PDFs, wikis, and Slack threads. A reliable RAG system could make this knowledge instantly searchable.

Rixvol constructs low-latency execution engines, tick-level historical market data lakes, and bias-free backtesting frameworks engineered for proprietary trading desks, market makers, and quantitative research teams.
