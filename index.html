Polymarket Trading Bot Development

High-Frequency Market-Making, Sub-Second News Sniping, and Automated CLOB Execution

Algorithmic Foundation & Market-Making Edge

Polymarket has transformed prediction markets by transitioning from legacy Automated Market Makers (CPMM AMMs) to a hybrid, off-chain Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) with on-chain settlement on Polygon. Rixvol designs custom high-speed trading engines that operate directly on this CLOB architecture, quoting tight two-sided markets, absorbing volume, and earning maker rebates without incurring on-chain gas costs for order placement or cancellations.

Our Polymarket bots implement modified Avellaneda-Stoikov volatility-spread equations adapted for bounded binary outcome assets ($0.00 ≤ P ≤ $1.00). Rather than relying on simple linear order books, the pricing engine models inventory risk dynamically—adjusting reservation quotes as inventory skews toward either "YES" or "NO" outcomes to guarantee continuous risk-neutrality.

Avellaneda-Stoikov Binary Inventory Equation ADAPTED FOR BINARY TOKENS
r(s, q, t) = s - q × γ × σ² × (T - t) × [s × (1 - s)]
Where s is the current midpoint probability, q is net outcome token inventory, γ is the fund's risk aversion coefficient, and s(1 - s) incorporates binary variance bounds.

Kalshi Trading Bot Development

CFTC-Regulated Event Execution, Macro Release Sniping, and Fedwire Automated Clearing

Design for Regulated Institutional Event Derivatives

Kalshi is a Designated Contract Market (DCM) regulated by the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). Unlike decentralized smart contracts, Kalshi requires strict compliance with US regulatory regimes, RSA 4096-bit cryptographic API request signatures, precise position accountability limits, and bank-grade wire settlement in pure US Dollars.

Rixvol's Kalshi bot engines are purpose-built for macroeconomic event contract trading: US CPI, nonfarm payrolls (NFP), FOMC interest rate announcements, GDP forecasts, and target treasury spreads. The bots incorporate our proprietary Macro Release Sniping Module—synchronizing with federal agency data clocks down to the microsecond, parsing direct government RSS/JSON feeds, and executing trades before the broader market reprices the curve.

The Rixvol engine extracts core CPI numbers in under 12ms, evaluates implied contracts vs actual figures, cancels existing maker quotes on stale price levels, and fills Kalshi Yes contracts across 4 target strike bands before latency arbitrageurs can sweep the book.

Cross-Platform Trading Analytics

Multi-Venue Discrepancy Matrices, Synthetic Dutch-Book Engines, and Fee-Adjusted Arbitrage Signals

Pure Arbitrage: Polymarket vs Kalshi vs PredictIt

Due to segmented capital pools, regulatory firewalls, and geographical restrictions, identical real-world event contracts trade at widely divergent prices across exchanges. For example, a contract predicting a 25bps interest rate cut may trade at $0.62 on Polymarket (crypto native) while simultaneously trading at $0.67 on Kalshi (US dollar native).

Rixvol's Cross-Platform Trading Analytics continuously maps matching market contracts across venues, computes exact transaction costs (including exchange maker/taker fees, gas fees, and fiat withdrawal friction), and triggers instant risk-free basis arbitrage signals. It also computes Synthetic Dutch Books where buying Yes on Venue A and No on Venue B guarantees a positive payoff regardless of event outcome.

Whenever Spread > 0, purchasing both legs locks in guaranteed deterministic capital yield at contract settlement.
