Sports Betting Odds Aggregator

Data Feeds

Institutional-grade infrastructure that ingests, normalizes, and streams real-time odds from over 120 sharp and retail sportsbooks. Our proprietary entity resolution maps naming variants across 45+ languages into canonical IDs. Every update is delta-compressed via Protobuf and broadcast with sub-18ms latency — detecting stale lines, ghost odds, and coordinated steam moves in real time.

Multi-Format Normalization

American, Decimal, Fractional odds unified across 350+ market types.

Exchange Liquidity

Back/Lay depth from Betfair, Matchbook, Smarkets for optimal execution.

Stale Odds Detection

Cross-references tick intervals against atomic timestamps. Flags ghost lines in <50ms.

Multi-Protocol Delivery

gRPC + Protobuf, WebSocket + JSON, REST. SDKs for Go, Rust, Python, Node.js, Java.

EV+ Betting Analytics Software

Quant Pricing

Quantitative pricing engine that strips bookmaker margins using Shin's algorithm to find true fair probabilities. Scans 850K outcome pairs per second, flagging every positive expected value opportunity. Computes Kelly Criterion stake sizing, tracks Closing Line Value attribution, and generates Sharpe Ratio performance analytics.

Shin Demarginalization

Strips overround accounting for insider volume and favorite-longshot bias.

Real-Time EV Scanning

850K evaluations/sec across all connected bookmakers with configurable thresholds.

Kelly Criterion Sizing

Optimal fractional stakes for logarithmic growth. Quarter, half, and custom Kelly.

CLV Performance

Tracks Closing Line Value, Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, and max drawdown.
